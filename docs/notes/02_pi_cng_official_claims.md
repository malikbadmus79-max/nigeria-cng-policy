# Reading 2: Pi-CNG & EV official claims (2026) [S01, S08, S09]

## 5-line summary

1. The programme reports 120,000+ vehicles converted, 90 stations in 28 states, 7,700 technicians trained and 10,000+ jobs (2026 Term Report, May 2023 – Jan 2026).
2. It says it has met its 120,000-conversion target, though only 93,845 conversion kits were deployed against the same target.
3. A new chair (since 31 Oct 2025) is rolling out a 100,000 Conversion Kit Programme and "Convert Now, Pay Small Small" financing.
4. The Presidency promised CNG-driven fare cuts from 1 October 2026 and says 1,000 stations have been ordered, including a 15-station network in Lagos.
5. The official line is that perception and safety fears, not economics, are the main barrier to adoption (S05).

## What matters for our project

- **The official story frames the problem as demand** (people are afraid or unaware). Our hypothesis frames it as **supply of refuelling access**. The paper should test both explanations openly.
- **Targets are moving:** 1m by 2027, then 120k as a term target, then "220,000" in recent coverage. We should state clearly which target we measure against.
- **Station counts conflict** between 28 states (S01) and 23 states (S11) for the same 90 stations.

## Questions to follow up

- How does Pi-CNG count a "converted vehicle"? Does it include factory-built CNG vehicles?
- Is there a public list of station locations?
